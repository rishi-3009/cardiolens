- 2026-10-10 · Rishi · **Uncertainty range (A1)** = 10th–90th percentile of 20 models, each trained on a random 80% of patients (same model type; forests use 50 trees). The ranges share the main model's input preparation and are widened to always contain the main prediction. · Typical width is ~7 points (logistic regression) to ~18 points (forests), which is visible, unlike the ±2–5 that cross-validation fold models would give. **Ranges do NOT shrink step by step** (LAD: 13 points at step 1, 18 at step 5). They measure model wobble, not missing information, so Direction D's demo should rely on the % and colours changing instead.
